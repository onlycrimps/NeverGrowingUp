The Million Dollar Sentra

I have been meaning to start a blog of my life experiences for a long time.

There always seemed to be a reason not to. There was something else to do, somewhere else to go, or some better time to start.

So naturally, I decided to start with a Nissan Sentra.

The funny thing about old cars is that they have a way of becoming more than just cars. They become projects, problems, memories, and eventually stories.

This one has already managed to become all four.

I don't really know where this blog is going yet. That's probably part of the point.

There are a lot of things I want to write about — cars, trips, mistakes, people, places, and all the little things that seem insignificant until years later when they're the things you remember.

So here we are.
