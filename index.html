Never
Growing Up.

Thoughts, adventures, machines, money, mistakes, and the strange experience of trying to make a life worth remembering.

Growing older is inevitable. Growing up is optional.

Somewhere between figuring it out and refusing to.

This is a place for the things that don't fit neatly anywhere else. The road trips. The broken machines. The good ideas and terrible ones. The questions that don't have easy answers.

There is a little bit of FIRE in here, too — the desire for freedom, independence, and enough time to actually live. But money is only part of the story.

Mostly, this is about paying attention. Staying curious. And trying not to lose the parts of ourselves that made being a kid so damn interesting.
