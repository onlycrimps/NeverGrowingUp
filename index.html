About this place

I don't want to grow up. I just want to grow.

Never Growing Up is my little corner of the internet.

There will probably be some FIRE and personal finance. There will probably be cars and machines. There will definitely be adventures.

But underneath all of that is a simpler idea: life is short, the world is interesting, and I don't want to lose my curiosity just because I'm getting older.
