This isn't really a brand. It's not a business. It's just a place on the internet where I can put things I've been thinking about.

Sometimes that's a story from the road. Sometimes it's a broken machine sitting in the garage. Sometimes it's money, work, or the strange question of what we're actually supposed to be doing with our limited time here.

Mostly, it's about staying curious and trying not to lose the parts of ourselves that made the world interesting in the first place.

I don't want to grow up. I just want to grow.

Getting older seems unavoidable. Becoming boring isn't.

Never Growing Up is my little corner of the internet for documenting the experiment.

There will probably be some FIRE and personal finance here. There will probably be cars. There will definitely be adventures.

But underneath all of it is one bigger question: how do we build a life that we actually want to be awake for?
